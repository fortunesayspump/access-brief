# Research and rationale

Checked 17 November 2026.

## Field check

[Challenge submissions](https://github.com/hyper-elements/hyper-elements/discussions) included hospitality, payment receipt, launch, festival, wedding, vulnerability disclosure, sports and résumé templates. A practical access guide was not visible in the current listing. This is a limited snapshot, not a claim of exclusive originality.

## References

- [AccessAble](https://www.accessable.co.uk/): detailed information lets a visitor judge suitability for themselves. Adapted the needs-first information hierarchy, not visual assets or survey data.
- [GOV.UK accessible document guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/publishing-accessible-documents): HTML first; plain language, meaningful headings, single-column flow and non-color cues. Adapted these standards to the three output formats. The PDF is explicitly not certified accessible.
- [Smithsonian NMNH accessibility](https://naturalhistory.si.edu/visit/accessibility): first-party source for the example's six published facts and access enquiry phone. Statements are paraphrased, not copied wholesale. Source page accessed on 17 November 2026.

## Source mapping

Arrival uses the source's Entrance and Parking sections. Inside uses Information for Visitors in a Wheelchair, Courtesy Wheelchairs and Information for Visitors Who Are Deaf or Hard of Hearing. Quieter visit uses its When to Visit section. No current lift status or dimensional survey is claimed. No onsite inspection took place.

## Product decisions

No access score or universal suitability badge. A date is not a live-state guarantee. Unknown information stays unknown. Do not ask users to disclose their disability: readers can assess the details privately. No analytics or third-party tracking. Real information and a blank are both included to demonstrate honest incomplete states.

## Visual system

Single continuous reading column, moderate heading scale, clear grouping by visit stage. No pill badges, decorative left bars, invented map, generic icons, gradients or motion. Dark plum and purple distinguish this wayfinding-oriented template from the blue operational design of Signal Desk. Contrast tests cover all text colors on white; yellow focus is an outline, not body text.
